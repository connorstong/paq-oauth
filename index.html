Paq

Paq is a privately operated personal assistant built with OpenClaw. It helps its owner review email, manage calendars, and work with their Google Workspace data.

Google access is authorized by the account owner through OAuth. Depending on the permissions granted, Paq can read and organize Gmail, prepare drafts, send approved messages, read and manage calendar events, and access Drive files, Docs, Sheets, and contacts for requested tasks.

Paq is intended only for its owner’s personal accounts. This website describes the assistant; it does not provide a public sign-up service or access to account data.

Privacy policy

For support, use the support email displayed on Paq’s Google authorization screen.
