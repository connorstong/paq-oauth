Paq Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Purpose and data access

Paq is its owner’s personal assistant, built with OpenClaw. With the account owner’s OAuth authorization, it accesses Google data needed for requested tasks and configured automations. This may include Gmail messages and metadata, calendar events, contacts, Drive files, Google Docs, and Google Sheets. Access depends on the permissions granted.

Use and processing

Google data is used to provide the assistant’s requested features, such as email summaries, drafts, calendar management, reminders, and document assistance. Relevant content may be sent to OpenAI, the configured AI provider, to process those tasks. The assistant’s configuration can change; this policy must be updated when its data practices change.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored on the owner-controlled host using an encrypted credential store. Retrieved content and task outputs may also appear in local conversation history, logs, memories, and backups. They remain until deleted by the owner or by the host’s configured retention settings. External processing is subject to the AI provider’s applicable service terms and data-retention settings.

Sharing

The operator does not sell Google data or use it for advertising. Relevant data may be disclosed to the configured AI provider for task processing. When the owner requests an external action, such as sending an approved email, sharing a document, or inviting someone to a calendar event, the relevant information is disclosed to the specified recipients.

Google API Limited Use

Paq’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Control and deletion

The account owner can revoke Paq’s authorization through Google Account connections. Revocation stops further API access; it does not automatically erase existing local records. The owner controls deletion of local credentials, histories, logs, memories, and backups.

Contact

For questions about Paq or this policy, use the support email displayed on its Google authorization screen.
